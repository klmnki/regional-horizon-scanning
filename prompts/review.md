# Review accumulated evidence

## Weekly: preceding seven days

Use canonical records and source links. Compare the same economic mechanism across territories. Identify convergence, divergence, contradictory evidence, missing groups, language/source bias and places that received little coverage. Distinguish independent cases from repeated coverage of one event. Do not treat a higher headline count as stronger impact.

For each proposed emerging issue or uncertainty, state the precise geography, supporting IDs, alternative explanations, confidence and what would weaken the interpretation. Test at least two plausible directions for an uncertainty. Check whether a global finding's local transfer assumptions have gained or lost support. Leave unresolved questions visible.

## Monthly: first working day

Review accumulated emerging issues, drivers and uncertainties. Retain, strengthen, weaken, split, reframe or retire each interpretation with an evidence-linked reason. Drivers need a pattern across time and causal reasoning. Identify what changed in the mechanism, not just what was announced.

Check evidence that challenges the dominant story. Reassess overrepresented sources/sectors and neglected countries/languages. Select concrete observations that would help distinguish competing explanations in the next month. Keep human review status explicit.

Include the review in the normal briefing. Do not create another automation or send extra messages without authorization.
