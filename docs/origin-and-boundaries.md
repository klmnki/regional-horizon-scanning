# Origin and boundaries

Prepared on 13 November 2026 from an inspection of the current AI × Economy automation prompt, the live Scan Method and Territories & Sources tabs, signal/product/synthesis/coverage headers, and a bounded sample of saved coverage records. The October connected-shocks sprint was inspected to distinguish it from the selected workflow; it is outside this kit's scope.

## What the inspection establishes

The configured workflow has eight daily lanes, local-language requirements, bounded discovery windows, a selection rubric, economic mechanism coding, a cumulative evidence register, synthesis rules, and briefing/verification instructions. Sample saved coverage rows show queries, languages, selected IDs, gaps and recorded delivery verification. These support the description of how the workflow is operated.

This inspection was not a fresh source audit of every signal, a test of every scheduled run, or independent confirmation of every recorded email. The current source automation was marked active when inspected. Its state and future performance may change.

## What is reproduced

- The country, regional and analytical lanes, language strategy, selection ceilings and rubric.
- Separation of observation, mechanism, alternative explanations and interpretation.
- Stable evidence IDs, canonical-event deduplication, synthesis traceability and honest coverage states.
- A reusable daily prompt, review prompt, configuration and blank register definitions.

## What is new in this kit

- The offline Python initializer, prompt generator, structural validator, briefing renderer and append-only local archive.
- A Candidates template and explicit local JSON envelope around register rows.
- Fictional fixtures and automated tests.
- Normalised coverage/synthesis headers and generic policy/framing labels.

These tools make the method easier to demonstrate and check; they were not the original research engine. The original engine was a scheduled, tool-using assistant.

## What is excluded

No private framing text, original workbook identifiers, correspondence, raw delivery details, recipient addresses, source corpus, live signal records or credentials are distributed. Original registers, automations and frozen legacy snapshots are unchanged. This kit neither submits findings nor grants permission to send email or publish research.

No affiliation, endorsement or programme approval is implied. Research sources retain their own rights and access conditions. Users must decide what they are authorised to collect, process and share.
